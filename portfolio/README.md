## Quality bar for future additions

- Clear problem statement, scope, assumptions and intended audience.
- Architecture diagram and explanation of key decisions.
- Reproducible steps using synthetic data.
- Security, privacy, resilience and operational considerations.
- Validation steps, known limitations and lessons learned.
- No customer/employer confidential information, production details, credentials or personal data.
